CollabNotes

Real-time collaborative notes, built on Spring Boot & STOMP.

Notes that move at the speed of your team.

Open a page, share it, type. Each keystroke, each cursor, each person — synced live across every window, with no plugins and no setup.

Create an account Log in

Live editing

STOMP over WebSocket pushes every keystroke to everyone on the page — no manual refresh.

Workspaces & pages

Group pages into workspaces. Invite teammates by email with EDITOR or VIEWER roles.

Persistent

Every save hits the database — close the tab and come back to the same content.

Tip: register two accounts in separate windows, invite one to the other's workspace, open the same page in both, and watch it sync. Backend hosted on Railway, frontend on Vercel.
